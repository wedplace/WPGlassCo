Educate & Elevate

Sources We Trust

At WPGlassCo, we believe education is a major part of the cannabis experience. These resources have been selected because they reflect qualities we value including transparency, craftsmanship, consistency, consumer education, and overall experience.

Kache

Kache offers a wide range of products with a focus on quality, presentation, and customer experience. Their discreet packaging, efficient shipping, and broad selection make them a resource worth exploring.

Crysp

Crysp is recognized for rosin and flower selections with a strong reputation for premium presentation and consumer experience. Their California roots and attention to quality have made them a notable name within the cannabis community.

Rosslyn

Rosslyn focuses on quality rosin and concentrates while maintaining approachable options for different budgets. Their balance of quality and accessibility makes them a valuable resource.

White Label Exotics

White Label Exotics focuses on premium concentrates and flower with elevated packaging and detailed product information. Their emphasis on sourcing, cultivation details, and transparency helps consumers better understand their options.

Pine Park

Created by cannabis content creators, Pine Park combines education, creativity, and premium products. Their attention to packaging, ordering experience, flower, rosin, and quality management creates a unique consumer experience.

OnlyGas

OnlyGas combines cannabis culture with accessibility through quality flower selections and budget-conscious options. Their focus on maintaining quality while offering different price points makes them a resource worth exploring.

Southern Harvest Hemp

Southern Harvest Hemp focuses on organic, small-batch production and high-quality concentrates. Their emphasis on craftsmanship and local production aligns with the values we look for in trusted resources.

Mood

Mood offers a curated cannabis experience with a focus on edibles and flower. Their discreet packaging, consistent product experience, and intentional approach to helping consumers find products that fit their preferences make them a unique resource.

Our Commitment to Transparency

WPGlassCo is a Texas-based glass company focused on craftsmanship, education, and community. We do not sell cannabis products, operate as a cannabis marketplace, or represent any of the companies listed on this page.

The companies featured here are not sponsored placements, paid advertisements, affiliate links, or partnerships. They are included because we believe they provide valuable resources and experiences for consumers interested in learning more about cannabis and hemp products.

Cannabis laws, product availability, and companies change frequently. This list is reviewed and updated regularly to provide Texans with access to educational resources and information.

Always research products, understand applicable laws, and make informed decisions based on your own needs and circumstances.
