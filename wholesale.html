Local Partnerships. Real Relationships.

We believe wholesale should be more than sending out boxes. WPGlassCo focuses on building relationships with local retailers who value quality products, education, and community.

Why Partner With WP?

📍 Local Only

Our wholesale program is focused on building strong local partnerships with retailers we can personally support.

💰 $300 Minimum Order

WPGlassCo is built around craftsmanship, knowledge, and connection. We want our partners to feel confident carrying our products.
