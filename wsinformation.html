WHOLESALE INFORMATION

Your WP Partner
Guide.

Everything you need to know about partnering with WPGlassCo. From requirements and ordering to support and resources, we're here to help you build a successful partnership.

Built Around Real Partnerships

WPGlassCo believes wholesale should be more than a transaction. We focus on building relationships with local retailers who share our appreciation for quality products, craftsmanship, and education.

Our goal is to support our partners with great products, knowledge, and resources that help create better customer experiences.

Wholesale Requirements

Local Partnerships

Our wholesale program is currently focused on local retailers where we can provide direct support and personal connection.

$300 Minimum Order

Wholesale partnerships begin with a $300 minimum opening order.

Retail Environment

Partners should provide a customer-focused retail experience that aligns with WP values.

Shared Values

We partner with businesses that value quality, education, and responsible product knowledge.

Partner Benefits

Approved WP partners receive more than inventory. We provide resources designed to help your team understand and confidently present our products.

E&E Guides

Exclusive Educate & Elevate selling guides to support staff education and customer conversations.

Direct Support

Local communication, delivery coordination, and partnership support from WP.

Frequently Asked Questions

What is the wholesale minimum?

Our wholesale program begins with a $300 minimum opening order.

Do you personally deliver orders?

Yes. WP focuses on local partnerships and direct support whenever possible.

Do partners receive education materials?

Yes. Approved partners receive access to exclusive Educate & Elevate selling resources.

How do I become a partner?

Submit an application and our team will follow up with next steps.
