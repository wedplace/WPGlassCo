Add WP Community Photo

Where It Started

We started Weedplace because we saw something missing.

As cannabis enthusiasts navigating the Texas market, we experienced the uncertainty firsthand. Finding quality products, understanding what was worth buying, and separating craftsmanship from mass-produced options wasn't always easy.

We wanted to create the place we wished existed: a space where people could explore, learn, and feel confident in their choices.

What WP Represents

Quality

We believe products should have purpose. Every piece, accessory, and tool should provide real value and be something worth owning.

Education

Through Educate & Elevate, we're creating resources that help people understand cannabis products, terminology, and culture.

Community

WP is built around the people — enthusiasts, collectors, artists, creators, and newcomers who all bring something unique to the culture.

Connection

We want Weedplace to be a place where people can share, discover, learn, and grow together.

Building Something Bigger

WP Glass Co is where our journey begins, but Weedplace represents the bigger vision.

Our goal is to create a destination where cannabis enthusiasts can shop, learn, connect, and celebrate the culture together.

Every product, article, conversation, and community interaction brings us closer to that future.
