THE PEOPLE BEHIND WP GLASS CO

Built by Passion.
Driven by Purpose.

WP Glass Co began with two people who loved cannabis culture, spent weekends exploring smoke shops together, and believed the community deserved something more thoughtful.

Our Story

WP Glass Co started the same way many great ideas do—with conversations. As cannabis consumers in Texas, we spent years navigating an industry that's constantly changing. Every new law, every new product, every new shop meant another opportunity to learn.

We loved visiting smoke shops together, but after a while we noticed a pattern. Too many shelves were filled with products that looked identical, and quality didn't always match the price.

Instead of settling for "good enough," we started imagining what we'd build if we could do it ourselves.

That idea eventually became WP Glass Co.

What We Believe

Quality Over Quantity

Every product we offer is selected because we'd proudly use it ourselves.

Education Matters

We believe informed customers make better decisions, which is why education is a core part of our brand.

Built for Community

WP Glass Co exists because of the people who share our passion for cannabis culture.

Always Growing

We're constantly learning, improving, and expanding what we offer.

Meet the Team

Sam

Industry Knowledge & Product Curation

Sam brings years of experience within the cannabis industry and a deep understanding of what customers and retailers are actually looking for. His knowledge helps shape every collection, ensuring quality, function, and value remain at the heart of WP Glass Co.

Chelsey

Creative Director & Cannabis Enthusiast

Chelsey is the creative force behind WP Glass Co's identity. From website development and branding to photography, product presentation, and customer experience, she focuses on creating a brand that feels intentional, memorable, and authentic.

More Than a Store.

WP Glass Co is the result of two people combining their strengths to build something meaningful. Every product, every page, and every customer interaction reflects our commitment to quality, creativity, and the community that inspired it all. Thank you for being part of our journey.
